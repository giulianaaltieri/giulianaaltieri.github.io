Terna Driving Energy

Beyond what you see, beyond what you know.

Type
Internal communication · Identity
Context
Havas PR · Milan
Role
Communication concept & identity
Year
2025 — 26

Goal

Strengthen the relationship between the company and its employees.

Idea

Three formats, three identities, one shared visual system.

Delivered

Logos and materials for posters, social posts and quizzes.

01

The goal

An internal communication proposal for Terna, designed to strengthen the bond with employees and build a sense of belonging.

02

The idea

The proposal is built on three formats — company storytelling, a multi-stop journey across locations and an internal competition — each with its own identity, connected by shared visual elements.

03

What I did

  1. 01

    Three connected logos

    A dedicated logo for each format, linked by shared visual elements.

  2. 02

    Applications

    Posters, social posts and quizzes built on the system.

04

The result

A flexible identity system: three distinct formats that are immediately recognisable as part of the same family.
