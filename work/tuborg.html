Tuborg Italia

Form, rhythm, and a presence in constant motion.

Type
Key visual · Event
Context
Havas PR · Milan
Role
Key visual & touchpoints
Year
2025 — 26

Goal

Bring the brand to life at a music festival.

Idea

A key visual and a call to action — #suonafresco — that move like music.

Delivered

One concept applied across physical and digital touchpoints.

01

The goal

Create Tuborg’s presence at a music festival, in line with the brand’s identity, and turn it into something people want to interact with and share.

02

The idea

A key visual built on form and rhythm, with the call to action #suonafresco: a presence in constant motion, like the music around it.

03

What I did

  1. 01

    Key visual & call to action

    Concept and development of the festival key visual and of #suonafresco.

  2. 02

    Physical touchpoints

    Roll-ups, a mirror installation, a tattoo vending machine and a 360° platform.

  3. 03

    Digital formats

    Video formats for Instagram stories.

04

The result

A single concept that holds together across very different formats, from a roll-up to a 360° platform, creating an immersive experience aligned with the spirit of the festival.
